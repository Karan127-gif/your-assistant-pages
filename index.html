Your Assistant

Your Assistant is an AI-powered personal productivity application that helps users manage their daily tasks through natural language conversation.

What Your Assistant does

  • Calendar management — create and view Google Calendar events by simply typing what you need (e.g. "schedule a meeting tomorrow at 5pm")
  • Email summaries — get quick summaries of recent unread Gmail messages without opening your inbox
  • Reminders — set reminders that are delivered to you via email at the scheduled time

How it works

Users sign in with their Google account, which allows the app to securely access their Calendar and Gmail on their behalf. All actions are performed only when explicitly requested by the user through the chat interface.
